## Decisions

- `escape_street`, `jolly`: dead neighbour writes removed, `partial_patches`
  entries removed. They now log `applied`. Runtime evidence above.
- `moon`, `peta`: keep the geometry, keep the `applied_partial` marker and the
  dead writes until the missing link is re-expressed.
  - `moon` geometry check: quad 2777 (seg 23) borders `(2200,50)-(2250,100)`,
    quad 2758 (seg 22) borders `(2125,0)-(2225,50)`. The appended door runs
    `(2200,50)->(2225,50)`, exactly on the shared edge. Visibility test
    (second pass) shows `see_fwd=1 see_rev=1` after the cross-link, but
    `navpath 22->23 ok=0 direct=0`: visibility is **not** the gate. Accepted
    loss (`drop-log.md`); block stays `applied_partial`.
  - `peta` geometry check: quad 121 (seg 47) borders `(975,-9725)-(2100,-9700)`,
    quad 126 (seg 53) borders `(1275,-9825)-(1375,-9725)`. Door 201 runs
    `(1275,-9725)->(1375,-9725)`, exactly on the shared edge. Same result:
    `see=1/1` after cross-link, `navpath 47->53 ok=0`. Accepted loss; the
    working 47<->91 door is kept.
- `skm_mallcrasher`: the standard Vlad job (`mallcrasher`,
  `narratives/vlad/mallcrasher`, CRC `75811735`) and the skirmish stage
  (`skm_mallcrasher`, `narratives/skm/skm_mallcrasher`, CRC `598935744`) are

  `applied_partial`; the standard job logs `skip_nopatch`.

## Harness bug found

`TH.p7_dump` iterated `plan.paths` unconditionally; the Mallcrasher plan has
no path pairs, so `ipairs(nil)` raised and the block ended with
`p7auto ERROR nil`. All dumps before the error were complete. Fixed with
`plan.dumps/doors/paths or {}`.
